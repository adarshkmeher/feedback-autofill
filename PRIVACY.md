# Privacy policy: IIT KGP ERP Feedback Autofill

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This extension is an unofficial tool. IIT Kharagpur and the ERP team did not make it.

## What the extension stores

- The ratings and comments you type on the extension's settings page, and the settings you choose. They are stored with `chrome.storage.sync`, so Chrome keeps them in your browser profile and may sync them across your own signed-in devices.
- A draft of the answers on the open feedback form, and a list of which subject and teacher forms you submitted in that tab. They are stored in the page's `sessionStorage` and disappear when you close the tab.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, send, sell or share any data. It has no server, no analytics and no tracking.
- It makes no network request of its own. It runs only on `https://erp.iitkgp.ac.in/Academic/studentFeedbackResponseView.htm`.
- It does not read, store or type your security code, and it does not press Submit.
- It loads no remote code.

The only data that leaves your browser is what you submit yourself on the ERP form, which goes to the ERP as it would without the extension.

## Your control

Remove the extension in `chrome://extensions` to delete its stored answers. You can also clear them any time with "Restore defaults" on the settings page.
